Privacy Policy

2026-10-15

The short version

Your training, food and body information stays on your phone. Fortavola (FT) has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. We never sell or share your personal information.

Who we are

Fortavola is published by Fortavola, Brisbane, Queensland, Australia ("we", "us"). Contact: fortavola.app@gmail.com.

Information stored only on your phone

When you use the app it saves the following on your device. We cannot see it and it is not sent to us:

  • profile: name, language, units, age, sex, height and weight (used only to calculate training and food targets)
  • training: goal, level, equipment, plan, the sets, weights and reps you log, rest timers
  • food: meals and foods you log, meal plans, shopping lists, body-weight entries, foods you choose to avoid
  • safety answers: that you confirmed you are 18 or older and whether you said a health condition applies to you (not which one)

Some of this is health information. It stays under your control on your device and in any backup file you choose to create.

Information processed when you buy Fortavola Plus

Payments are handled by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We never see your card or bank details.

To confirm whether your subscription is active, the app uses RevenueCat, Inc. (USA) as our service provider. RevenueCat receives a random app user ID created by the app, your purchase and subscription history, and basic technical data (platform, app version, country and IP address). It does not receive your name, email, health, training or food information.

We use this only to provide Plus (contract) and to keep records required by tax law (legal obligation).

Information the app stores collect

Google and Apple may collect information such as installs, crash reports you agree to share, and purchase records under their own privacy policies. We only see summary statistics from them.

If you contact us

If you email us, we use your email address and message only to reply, and delete them within 24 months.

Health information

We do not collect, store or process your health information on any server. If we ever offer a feature that needs to send any of it off your phone (for example an AI coach), it will be optional, it will ask for your separate, explicit consent first, and this policy will be updated before it launches.

Notifications and permissions

Rest-timer alerts are scheduled on your phone; no data leaves the device. The app does not use your location, contacts, camera, microphone or advertising ID.

Backups

Backup files are created only when you ask. They are not encrypted, so keep them somewhere private. Where you save or send them is your choice.

Where information is processed

RevenueCat, Google and Apple may process purchase information in the United States and other countries. They use recognised safeguards, such as Standard Contractual Clauses, for international transfers.

How long we keep information

Information on your phone is kept until you delete it. Purchase records are kept while your subscription is active and then for as long as tax law requires (in Australia, 5 years). Support emails are deleted within 24 months.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the Australian Privacy Principles, the EU and UK GDPR, the Saudi and UAE personal data protection laws, or the data laws of Spain, Mexico, Argentina and Colombia), you can ask to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your information, object to or restrict its use, and withdraw consent.

  • Data on your phone: export it with Me → Backup, or erase everything with Me → Privacy and safety → Delete all data on this phone.
  • Purchase data held by RevenueCat: email fortavola.app@gmail.com with the purchase ID shown in Me → Privacy and safety. We will respond within 30 days.

You can also complain to your data protection authority, for example the OAIC (Australia), your EU supervisory authority such as the AEPD (Spain), the ICO (UK), SDAIA (Saudi Arabia), the AAIP (Argentina) or the SIC (Colombia).

Children

Fortavola is for adults aged 18 and over. It is not directed at children and we do not knowingly process children's information.

No selling, no advertising

We do not sell or share personal information, use it for advertising, or build profiles about you. This also applies to US state privacy laws, including consumer health data laws such as Washington's My Health My Data Act: we do not collect consumer health data.

Security

Your data is protected by your phone's own security (screen lock and encryption). Purchase data is encrypted in transit.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date below and show a notice in the app before the change takes effect when it matters to you.
