Health and safety

2026-10-15

Important

Fortavola is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any medical condition. Consult a doctor or other qualified health professional for medical advice, diagnosis or treatment.

Before you start, talk to a doctor if:

  • a doctor has told you that you have a heart condition, or you have chest pain, fainting or dizziness
  • you have high blood pressure, diabetes or another long-term condition
  • you are pregnant or have given birth in the last 12 months
  • you have a bone, joint or back problem, a recent injury or surgery
  • you take medicine that affects your heart rate or blood pressure
  • you have, or have had, an eating disorder
  • you are unsure whether exercise is safe for you

While you train

  • Warm up first and learn each movement with a light load.
  • Set up machines so they fit you, and use safety bars or a spotter for heavy free weights.
  • Stop straight away if you feel pain, chest pain, dizziness, nausea or unusual breathlessness.
  • In an emergency, call your local emergency number (000 in Australia, 112 in the EU, 911 in Mexico and the US, 997 in Saudi Arabia, 998 in the UAE).

Food

Calorie and protein targets are estimates for healthy adults and never go below a safe minimum. Allergen labels are a guide; check product labels. If food, weight or exercise starts to feel out of control, please reach out for support: in Australia, the Butterfly National Helpline 1800 33 4673 (8am–midnight AEST), or a health professional in your country.
